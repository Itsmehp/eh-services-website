# Call-to-Action Definitions for Service Pages

This document outlines proposed Call-to-Actions (CTAs) for each key service-related page on the EH Services website. The goal is to guide visitors towards desired actions, aligning with the overall objectives of lead generation and engagement.

---

## 1. /services

**Primary Goal:** Encourage users to explore specific services or request a consultation.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **Hero Section:** "Explore Our Services" (link to specific service categories or a detailed services overview)
*   **Service Category Blocks:** "Learn More about [Service Name]" (link to dedicated service page)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "Ready to Transform Your Digital Presence? Get a Free Consultation." (link to contact page/form)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "View Our Portfolio" (link to portfolio page)

---

## 2. /portfolio

**Primary Goal:** Showcase past work and encourage users to inquire about similar projects.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **Case Study Thumbnails:** "View Case Study" (link to individual case study page)
*   **Individual Case Study Page (end):** "Inspired by Our Work? Let's Discuss Your Project." (link to contact page/form)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "See All Our Success Stories" (link to portfolio overview)

---

## 3. /pricing

**Primary Goal:** Clarify service costs and encourage users to get a custom quote.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **Pricing Tiers:** "Get Started with [Tier Name]" (link to contact page/form, possibly pre-filled with service interest)
*   **Custom Quote Section:** "Need a Custom Solution? Request a Personalized Quote." (link to contact page/form)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "Not Sure Which Plan is Right? Contact Us for Guidance." (link to contact page)

---

## 4. /contact

**Primary Goal:** Facilitate direct communication.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **Form Submission Button:** "Send Message" or "Request Consultation"
*   **Alternative Contact Info:** "Call Us Today" (phone number), "Email Us" (email address)

---

## 5. /about

**Primary Goal:** Build trust and establish expertise, leading users to services.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **Team Section:** "Meet Our Experts" (internal anchor or link)
*   **Philosophy Section:** "Discover Our Approach to Web Success" (link to services page)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "Join Our Journey: Explore Career Opportunities" (link to careers if applicable, otherwise remove)
*   **Bottom of Page:** "Ready to Collaborate? Get in Touch." (link to contact page/form)

---

## 6. /faq

**Primary Goal:** Address common questions and guide users to next steps.

**Proposed CTAs:**
*   **End of FAQ:** "Still Have Questions? Contact Our Support Team." (link to contact page)
*   **Relevant Answers:** "Learn More about [Specific Service]" (link to relevant service page)

---
